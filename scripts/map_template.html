Moving = departure to next arrival, no station stop. Door-to-door includes time stopped in stations and is the like-for-like match to the scheduled speed, whose timetable already includes stops.

A breakdown is a train stopped at least the chosen number of minutes (dwell, or running time over schedule), cascading stops merged and credited to the frontmost station. Excludes terminal layovers, planned shutdowns, and habitual timed holds (mostly inbound Green Line).
